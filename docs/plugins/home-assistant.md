# Home Assistant

Your smart home on the desktop:

- Launcher › Home: your rooms and devices, scenes and scripts. Enter turns a light on or off, ←/→ dims it.
- A Home icon in the top bar: Shift+Enter in the launcher pins a device to its menu.
- Home Assistant itself as a web app.

The installer asks for your Home Assistant address (also one for Tailscale, if you use it) and a long-lived access token, which you make in Home Assistant under your profile › Security. Safe to run again to change them.

Homepage: https://www.home-assistant.io
Needs: a Home Assistant server
