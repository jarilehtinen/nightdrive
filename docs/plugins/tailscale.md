# Tailscale

A private network between your own devices: reach your home server, NAS or Home Assistant from anywhere, as if you were at home.

Nightdrive puts it in the top bar: the icon shows whether you're connected, and its menu turns Tailscale on and off and lists your devices, online and offline.

After installing, log in once in a terminal: `sudo tailscale up`.

Homepage: https://tailscale.com
Needs: a Tailscale account (free for personal use)
