# Deck

A fast Spotify player for the terminal, made to work with Nightdrive.

- Music in the launcher: your albums, curated lists and search, Enter plays.
- A player row on top of the launcher while music plays, and Ctrl+Space plays or pauses.
- A music icon and menu in the top bar, and the media keys.

The installer builds Deck from source. You need your own (free) Spotify developer app for its Client ID; Deck's README walks you through it.

Homepage: https://github.com/jarilehtinen/deck
Needs: Spotify Premium
Keys: ⌘M opens Deck
